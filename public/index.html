Workflow Process

Closed-Loop Municipal Resolution

Three deterministic stages ensuring complete civic accountability from report to verified disposal.

Stage 01
📸

Geotag & AI Audit

Resident snaps photo with live GPS. The platform computes an MD5 perceptual hash to eliminate duplicate reporting and runs AI classification.

➔
Stage 02
🛡️

GIS Routing & Dispatch

Point-in-polygon geometry calculates the exact Bokaro zone. The Zone Officer dispatches the nearest active Field Worker via WebSocket.

➔
Stage 03
🏆

Proof Audit & Credit Mint

Field worker uploads after-cleanup proof. Upon supervisor verification, cryptographic UrbanSync Credits are minted into the citizen's wallet.

Platform Core

Architectural Innovations

Engineered to eliminate municipal bottlenecks and fraudulent credit claims.

📍

Ray-Casting Polygon Triage

Automated 5-zone incident allocation across Bokaro using coordinate bounding-box validation, eliminating human routing delays.

🛡️

Cryptographic MD5 Deduplication

Every uploaded incident image generates an MD5 cryptographic fingerprint, immediately catching and preventing duplicate submissions.

⚡

WebSocket Real-Time Dispatch

Bi-directional Socket.io telemetry delivers instantaneous updates to field workers, zone supervisors, and citizen wallets.

🎁

Fraud-Resistant Credit Economy

Credits are issued only after dual-photo verification (before & after), redeemable for real-world municipal discounts and UPI vouchers.
